## Troubleshooting policy errors

### “Destination not working” / HTTP 404 (Android)

1. Open `https://finalyearkit.com/ads/final-year-projects` on your phone — it must show **200**, not 404.
2. If you saved the ad **before** Vercel finished deploying, Google cached a 404. Fix:
   - In Google Ads → **Ads** → edit the ad → change nothing → **Save** (forces a new destination check), or
   - **Policy manager** → issue → **Request review** after the URL works.
3. Final URL must be **exactly** `https://finalyearkit.com/ads/final-year-projects` (no typo, no `/projects`).

### “Enabling dishonest behavior” (still disapproved)

1. Final URL must stay on `/ads/final-year-projects` (minimal header/footer, integrity + “what we do not offer” on page).
2. Remove ad headlines/descriptions that imply turnkey submission (see lists above).
3. Pause keywords: `report`, `documentation`, `assignment help`.
4. Appeal with the text in **Policy appeal** — mention the ads landing URL and integrity blog.

### “Not allowed in India” (limited / under review)

Often paired with academic-cheating classification. Same fixes as dishonest behavior; re-review can take several days. Do not run traffic to the old `/final-year-projects` URL from ads until approved.
